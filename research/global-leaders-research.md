## Samuel Hahnemann

**Suggested role label:** Founder of Homeopathy

**Draft profile summary:**  
Christian Friedrich Samuel Hahnemann was a German physician best known as the founder of homeopathy. Born in Meissen in 1755, he studied medicine and later developed the principle commonly known as "like cures like." His major works include *Organon of the Healing Art* and *Materia Medica Pura*. His birthday, April 10, is observed by the homeopathic community as World Homeopathy Day.

**Useful details for profile card:**
- Born: 10 April 1755, Meissen, Saxony
- Died: 2 July 1843, Paris, France
- Known for: Founding homeopathy
- Key works: *Organon of the Healing Art*, *Materia Medica Pura*, *Chronic Diseases*

## George Vithoulkas

**Suggested role label:** Classical Homeopathy Educator & Researcher

**Draft profile summary:**  
George Vithoulkas is a Greek professor, teacher, author, and practitioner of classical homeopathy. He is associated with the International Academy of Classical Homeopathy and is widely known for his teaching, research work, and contributions to classical homeopathic education. He received the Right Livelihood Award in 1996 and has authored several books and teaching systems in homeopathy.
